This Commercial Lease Agreement is made and entered into between Blair & Associates, the Landlord, and Daniel Ryan, doing business as Brennan Holdings, the Tenant. 

The Landlord agrees to lease the industrial property located at 258 Richmond Ave, Houston, TX, to the Lessee for a period of five years. The term of this agreement shall begin on December 6, 2024, and will continue until December 6, 2029. 

The Tenant agrees to pay as compensation for the use of the premises the sum of $21,372.12 per month. 

Regarding adjustments and protections, the parties agree to the following terms:
1. Annual Adjustments: The monthly payments required under this lease shall be subject to annual adjustments based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI-indexed) to account for fluctuations in market conditions.
2. Initial Funds Held: Upon the execution of this lease, the Lessee shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $14,786.18. This money shall be held by Blair & Associates throughout the term of the tenancy to cover any potential damages to the property or defaults in payment. These funds will be returned to the Tenant within thirty days of the conclusion of the lease, provided the property is returned in good condition.

The Lessee acknowledges that they have inspected the premises and accept the property in its current state. Both parties agree to abide by all local ordinances governing the use of the industrial space.
