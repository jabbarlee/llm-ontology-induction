This Commercial Lease Agreement is made and entered into this day by Blair & Associates (the "Owner") and Alexandra Le, doing business as Maddox Partners (the "Tenant").

The Owner agrees to lease to the Lessee, and the Lessee agrees to lease from the Owner, the industrial property located at 690 Beltway 8 Frontage, Houston, TX. This premises consists of 25,696 square feet, including the use of 5 loading docks.

The term of this lease shall begin on July 23, 2024, and shall conclude one year later on July 23, 2025. The base payment for the occupancy of the property is set at $30,880.63.

Financial Terms:
The Tenant shall provide a sum of $30,398.41 to the Owner upon execution of this agreement to be held as protection against any potential damages or breaches of contract throughout the duration of the term. Furthermore, the base payment amount specified herein shall be subject to adjustment based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to account for annual inflationary changes, with said adjustment applied to the base rate effective on the anniversary of this agreement.

The Lessee agrees to use the premises exclusively for warehouse-logistics operations. Blair & Associates expects the Tenant to maintain the interior of the premises in good condition throughout the duration of the stay. All payments should be directed to the contact email provided by the owner.
