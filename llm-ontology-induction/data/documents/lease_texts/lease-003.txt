This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Lindsey Roman, as the owner, and Edward Novak, representing Higgins & Associates. The premises subject to this agreement are located at 109 Fannin St, Houston, TX, consisting of office space within the building.

The term of this lease shall commence on April 16, 2024, and will continue in full force for three years, concluding on April 16, 2027. 

The Lessee agrees to pay the owner the sum of $19,303.22 per month for the duration of the occupancy. Regarding the financial adjustments during the term, the monthly payment shall be adjusted annually based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to account for fluctuations in the market. Furthermore, the Tenant is required to provide a payment of $25,839.74 to be held as a good-faith fund against any damages or defaults. This amount shall be returned to the Tenant after the premises have been vacated and inspected, provided there are no outstanding claims against the Lessee.

Higgins & Associates shall use the property for the purpose of restaurant and food service operations. The Tenant acknowledges they have been provided with the necessary contact information for the owner at lindsey@owner17mail.com. 

This agreement represents the full understanding between Lindsey Roman and the Tenant.
