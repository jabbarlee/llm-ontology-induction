This commercial lease agreement is made this day by and between Megan Mcclain (the "Landlord") and Tammy Allison, representing the Huff Group (the "Tenant"). The property subject to this lease is the office building located at 614 Beltway 8 Frontage, Houston, TX. 

The Lessee agrees to occupy the premises for a term of one year, beginning on August 9, 2025, and concluding on August 9, 2026. The monthly payment for the use of the office space is $14,315.78.

Regarding the financial terms of this agreement, the Tenant shall provide a payment of $40,400.15 to be held by the Landlord for the duration of the occupancy to cover any damages or defaults under this lease; these funds shall be returned within a reasonable timeframe following the expiration of the term, provided the property is left in good order. Furthermore, the base monthly payment shall be subject to a CPI-indexed increase, which will be calculated and applied to the monthly payment amount based on the most recent Consumer Price Index data to account for cost-of-living adjustments throughout the term of this lease.

The Tenant agrees to maintain the premises in a professional manner. Any disputes regarding the interpretation of these terms shall be settled according to local statutes. Megan Mcclain and the Lessee have reviewed these terms and agree to abide by them for the duration of the residency.
