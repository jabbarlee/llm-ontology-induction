COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made by and between Rogers & Associates (the "Landlord") and Jerry Henderson, doing business as Wright & Associates (the "Tenant"). The Landlord agrees to lease to the Lessee, and the Lessee agrees to lease from the Landlord, the retail space located at 6835 Yale St, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall begin on December 5, 2022, and shall continue for a period of five years, concluding on December 5, 2027.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $41,540.29 as the monthly payment for the premises. Regarding future adjustments to this payment, there shall be no escalation of the base payment amount for the duration of this agreement; the rate remains fixed as stated herein. 

Upon the signing of this document, Jerry Henderson shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $24,967.32. This amount is to be held by Rogers & Associates as a guarantee against any damages to the property or failure by the Lessee to fulfill the obligations set forth in this agreement. Said funds shall be returned to the Tenant within a reasonable timeframe following the expiration of the lease, provided the premises are returned in satisfactory condition.

The Tenant, acting as a warehouse-logistics operator, accepts the property in its current state. All payments shall be made payable to the Landlord at the contact information provided by Rogers & Associates. This agreement represents the entire understanding between the parties regarding the occupation of the Yale Street property.
