This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into on this day between Matthew Gardner, the property owner, and Carlos Walls, representing the Caldwell Group. The parties agree to the rental of the retail storefront located at 3239 Kirby Dr, Houston, TX.

The Lessee agrees to occupy the space starting February 16, 2024, and this agreement shall remain in effect through February 16, 2027. The monthly payment for the use of the premises is set at $34,336.42. 

Regarding financial adjustments and initial obligations, the following terms shall apply:
1. Annual Payment Adjustments: The monthly base payment shall be subject to an annual increase based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to ensure the rate keeps pace with market inflation.
2. Initial Funds Held: Upon signing this agreement, the Tenant shall provide a total of $12,847.68 to be held by Matthew Gardner as a guarantee against any potential damages to the property or failure to perform under the terms of this contract. This sum will be returned to Carlos Walls within a reasonable timeframe after the premises are vacated in good order.

The Tenant is responsible for all activities conducted within the storefront, which operates within the medical industry. All notices regarding this lease should be directed to the contact information provided by both parties. Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be addressed according to standard commercial practices in the state of Texas.
