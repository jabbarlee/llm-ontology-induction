This Commercial Lease Agreement is made between Davis Partners (hereinafter the "Landlord") and Jesse Mckay, operating as Rodriguez & Associates, for the professional office space located at 1233 Bellaire Blvd, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall commence on April 27, 2023, and shall continue in full force and effect until April 27, 2026. The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $11,659.55 per month for the use of the premises.

Regarding the financial obligations and protections of this agreement, the parties agree to the following terms: 

1. Monthly Payment Adjustments: The Tenant agrees that the monthly payment obligation shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase of 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date throughout the duration of this lease.

2. Performance Guarantee: Upon the execution of this document, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $8,336.22 to be held by Davis Partners as a guarantee for the faithful performance of the terms of this lease. This amount may be applied by the Landlord to cover any damages to the property or unpaid balances upon the conclusion of the term.

The Lessee shall be responsible for all professional office activities conducted on the premises. Davis Partners agrees to provide access to the building and associated parking facilities as currently configured. This agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding the occupation of the office space.
