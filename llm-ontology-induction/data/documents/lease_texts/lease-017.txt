This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Megan Mcclain (the "Landlord") and Tammy Allison (the "Lessee"). The parties hereby agree to the leasing of the premises located at 614 Beltway 8 Frontage, Houston, TX.

The term of this agreement shall begin on August 9, 2023, and shall continue until August 9, 2025. Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $13,683.75 per month for the duration of the term. 

Regarding payments and financial security, the following terms shall apply:

1. Annual Adjustments: The monthly payment shall be subject to a CPI-indexed adjustment on each anniversary of the commencement date to account for changes in the consumer price index. 
2. Initial Payment: Upon execution of this lease, the Tenant shall provide a payment of $40,400.15 to the Landlord. This sum shall be held as a good-faith guarantee against any damage to the premises or default on the terms of this agreement, to be returned within a reasonable timeframe following the expiration of the lease, less any necessary deductions.

The Lessee shall use the office property solely for the purpose of the business known as Huff Group. Tammy Allison agrees to maintain the premises in good condition throughout the duration of the occupancy. Any notices to be served to the Landlord should be directed to megan@owner3mail.com, and notices to the Lessee shall be directed to contact29@huff.com. 

This agreement represents the entire understanding between Megan Mcclain and the Lessee.
