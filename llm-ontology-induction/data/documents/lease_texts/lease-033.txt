COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made by and between Abigail Shaffer, the Landlord, and Thomas Ramos, representing Ramirez Partners, the Tenant. The parties agree that the property located at 1036 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX shall be occupied by the Lessee for the purpose of retail business operations.

The term of this agreement shall begin on September 30, 2024, and shall conclude on September 28, 2025. In consideration for the use of the premises, Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a monthly payment of $20,641.51.

Terms regarding financial adjustments and protections:
1. Annual Adjustments: The base payment amount specified above is subject to a CPI-indexed escalation. This adjustment will be calculated based on the Consumer Price Index to reflect changes in the cost of living and maintenance expenses annually, effective on each anniversary of the term.
2. Performance Assurance: Upon signing this agreement, the Lessee shall provide the sum of $29,330.16 to be held as a good-faith payment to cover any potential damages to the property or failure to fulfill the terms of this agreement. This sum will be returned to Thomas Ramos within 30 days of the conclusion of the lease, provided the premises are returned in satisfactory condition and all obligations have been met.

The Lessee agrees to maintain the storefront as required for retail business and shall be responsible for all standard utilities incurred during the duration of the occupancy. Both Abigail Shaffer and the Tenant agree to abide by all local ordinances governing the use of the Westheimer Rd location. 

In witness whereof, the parties have executed this agreement as of the date first written above.
