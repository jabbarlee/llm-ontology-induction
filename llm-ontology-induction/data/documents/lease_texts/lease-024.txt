This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Javier Johnson (the Landlord) and Rebecca Henderson, doing business as Rodriguez & Associates, regarding the retail premises located at 1093 Studemont St, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall begin on May 27, 2024, and shall continue for a period of exactly two years, concluding on May 27, 2026. For the occupancy of the space, Tenant shall pay a total sum of $39,274.61.

Payment and Financial Terms:
The Lessee shall provide the Landlord with an initial sum of $4,162.24 upon the execution of this agreement. This amount shall be held as a good-faith payment to cover any potential damages to the property or defaults in the terms of this agreement, and shall be returned to the Tenant within a reasonable timeframe after the conclusion of the lease, provided the premises are returned in good order. Furthermore, the parties agree that the base payment obligation shall increase by a fixed percentage of 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.

Use of Premises:
The space is to be used solely for the purpose of operating an office-professional business. The Tenant agrees to abide by all rules and regulations set forth by the property management.

Miscellaneous:
This document constitutes the entire agreement between Javier Johnson and Rebecca Henderson. Any modifications to this lease must be made in writing and signed by both parties. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
