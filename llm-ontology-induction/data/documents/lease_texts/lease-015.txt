COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made and entered into this 30th day of August, 2024, by and between Allison Hill (hereinafter referred to as the "Landlord") and Tammy Allison, operating as the Huff Group (the "Tenant"). The Landlord hereby leases to the Lessee the premises located at 1263 Gessner Rd, Houston, TX, consisting of the office building and its associated parking area.

The term of this lease shall begin on August 30, 2024, and shall continue in full force and effect until August 28, 2029. 

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a sum of $13,145.24 per month as consideration for the use of the premises. Regarding future adjustments to this payment, the parties agree that the base payment shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase of 3% annually, which will be applied on each anniversary of the commencement date. 

Upon the signing of this document, the Lessee shall provide the Landlord with a payment in the amount of $38,114.10. This sum shall be held by Allison Hill as protection against any potential damages to the property or failure of the Tenant to perform their obligations under this agreement. This sum shall be returned to the Tenant following the conclusion of the lease term, provided that the premises are left in good condition and all accounts are settled.

The Lessee shall be responsible for all standard utility costs associated with the operation of the business. Both parties agree that this document serves as the binding agreement for the premises and that any future modifications to this arrangement may be handled via a separate amendment.
