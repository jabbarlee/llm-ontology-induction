This lease agreement is made and entered into by and between Robinson Partners (the "Landlord") and Eric Carney, doing business as Blair Holdings (the "Tenant"). The Landlord agrees to lease the industrial property located at 3446 Main St, Houston, TX, to the Lessee for a term beginning on September 2, 2029, and concluding on September 2, 2032.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a monthly payment of $22,271.94 for the use of the premises. 

Additional Terms:

1. Payment Increases: The parties agree that the base payment will be subject to a fixed percentage increase annually throughout the term of this agreement. The Landlord will provide written notice to the Tenant regarding the specific adjusted amount at least thirty days prior to the start of each new annual period.

2. Initial Funds: Upon signing this agreement, the Tenant shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $6,001.29 to be held as a guarantee for the faithful performance of the terms of this lease. This sum shall be returned to the Tenant within thirty days after the expiration of the lease, provided that the property is returned in good condition, minus any costs for necessary repairs beyond normal wear and tear.

The Tenant acknowledges that they have inspected the premises and accepts the property in its current condition. The Landlord and Eric Carney agree to be bound by the terms set forth herein, and this document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the property.
