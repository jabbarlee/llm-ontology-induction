This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Javier Johnson (the "Landlord") and Shawn Arroyo (the "Tenant"), representing the business Parker & Associates, regarding the office space located at 6047 Hempstead Rd, Houston, TX.

The Lessee agrees to occupy the premises for a term beginning on September 9, 2028, and concluding on September 9, 2031. During this period, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a monthly sum of $27,413.63 for the use of the property.

Payment and Financial Terms:
The monthly payment of $27,413.63 shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase of 3% on each anniversary of the start date of the agreement. Upon signing this document, the Lessee shall provide the Landlord with a sum of $15,066.62. This money will be held by the Landlord for the duration of the occupancy to cover any potential damages to the property or defaults in payment and will be returned to Shawn Arroyo following the conclusion of the term, provided the space is left in good condition.

The Tenant agrees to maintain the premises in accordance with standard professional office expectations. Any alterations to the internal layout must be approved in writing by Javier Johnson prior to commencement of work. This agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding the property at 6047 Hempstead Rd.
