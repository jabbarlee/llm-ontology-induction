This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Robinson Partners (the "Landlord") and Gerald Hensley, doing business as Garcia Enterprises (the "Tenant"). The Landlord agrees to lease to the Lessee the retail property located at 7554 Shepherd Dr, Houston, TX, consisting of 29222 square feet.

The term of this lease shall begin on February 11, 2023, and shall continue for a period of exactly one year, concluding on February 11, 2024. 

For the right to occupy the premises, the Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a total sum of $27,460.21. With respect to future payments, there shall be no scheduled increases, adjustments, or cost-of-living escalations applied to this amount throughout the term of this agreement. 

Upon the signing of this document, the Lessee shall provide to Robinson Partners the sum of $12,442.52 to be held as a damage and performance bond. This money will be held by the Landlord to cover any potential repairs or unpaid obligations at the conclusion of the term. Provided the property is returned in good condition, this sum will be returned to the Tenant within a reasonable timeframe after vacating the premises. 

The Tenant shall maintain the premises in good working order and shall be responsible for all daily operations associated with the business. Should any disputes arise regarding the terms of this lease, the parties agree to seek an amicable resolution through direct communication.
