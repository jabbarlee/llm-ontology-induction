COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Allison Hill (the "Landlord") and Tammy Allison (the "Tenant"), doing business as Huff Group, for the premises located at 1263 Gessner Rd, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall begin on August 28, 2029, and shall continue for exactly three years, terminating on August 28, 2032. The Lessee agrees to pay to the Landlord the sum of $13,985.51 per month as compensation for the use of the office space.

Regarding adjustments and protections, the following provisions shall apply:

1. Annual Adjustments: The base payment rate shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase of 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date throughout the term of this occupancy.

2. Performance Assurance: Upon execution of this agreement, the Tenant shall provide the sum of $38,114.10 to be held by Allison Hill. This amount shall be retained as a guarantee against any damages to the property or defaults in performance under the terms of this lease. Provided the Lessee has complied with all conditions herein, this sum shall be returned following the conclusion of the term.

The Tenant is granted access to the property for the purpose of warehouse-logistics operations. Both parties agree that this document constitutes the full understanding regarding the rental of the premises at 1263 Gessner Rd.
