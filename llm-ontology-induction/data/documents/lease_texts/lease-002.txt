This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Lindsey Roman, the landlord, and Mr. Philip Cannon, the Lessee, for the office space located at 3027 Heights Blvd, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease begins on June 2, 2024, and shall expire on June 2, 2025. During this period, the Tenant agrees to pay the landlord a total sum of $42,801.60 for the use of the premises.

The financial terms are as follows:
1. Payment Adjustments: The financial obligation set forth herein shall be subject to a CPI-indexed increase, which will be applied annually to reflect changes in the Consumer Price Index.
2. Initial Funds Held: Upon the execution of this document, the Lessee shall provide the landlord with the sum of $28,795.68. This amount shall be held by the landlord as a good faith guarantee against any potential damages or breaches of this agreement, to be returned at the conclusion of the term provided the premises are left in good condition.

Miller Partners shall occupy the premises for office-professional use. The Tenant is responsible for all utility payments associated with the space throughout the duration of the occupancy. Both parties agree that this document constitutes the full understanding regarding the property.

Signed,

Lindsey Roman 
Mr. Philip Cannon
