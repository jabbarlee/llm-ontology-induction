COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Walker Group, an LLC (the "Landlord"), and Tammy Allison, doing business as Huff Group (the "Tenant").

The Landlord hereby agrees to lease to the Lessee, and the Lessee agrees to lease from the Landlord, the office property located at 4046 Hempstead Rd, Houston, TX. This property consists of 38,682 square feet of space. The term of this agreement shall begin on March 14, 2023, and shall conclude on March 14, 2028. 

The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord as consideration for the use of the premises the sum of $36,130.62. Regarding the financial obligations of the Lessee, the parties agree as follows: There shall be no annual increase or escalation applied to the payment amount during the duration of this agreement. Furthermore, upon the execution of this contract, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $9,296.73 to the Landlord to be held as a good-faith deposit to ensure the performance of all terms herein, which shall be returned at the end of the term provided the premises are surrendered in good condition.

The parties acknowledge that this lease renews into a new term starting March 14, 2028, should the parties reach a subsequent agreement. 

In witness whereof, the parties have signed this document.
