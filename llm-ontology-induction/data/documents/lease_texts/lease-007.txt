This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Walker Group, an LLC, and Tammy Allison, representing Huff Group, regarding the office space located at 4046 Hempstead Rd, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall begin on March 14, 2023, and shall continue in full force until March 14, 2028. The Lessee agrees to pay the owner the sum of $36,130.62 for the use of the premises.

Regarding the financial terms of this agreement, the following clauses shall apply:

1. Payment Adjustments: There shall be no annual increase or other adjustments applied to the base payment amount for the duration of the term; the rate remains fixed as stated herein.

2. Damage/Performance Fund: Upon the signing of this document, the Tenant shall provide a payment of $9,296.73 to Walker Group. This sum will be held by the owner to ensure the faithful performance of the lease terms and to cover any potential damages to the property beyond normal wear and tear. This amount will be returned to the tenant after they vacate the premises, provided all terms have been met.

The Tenant, Tammy Allison, shall maintain the property in good order throughout the five-year occupancy. All communications regarding this lease should be directed to the Walker Group at walker@owner2mail.com or to the tenant at contact29@huff.com. 

This agreement supersedes any previous arrangements, including those referenced in amendment lease lease-008. By signing, the parties acknowledge their understanding of these terms and their intent to occupy the office space for the agreed-upon duration.
