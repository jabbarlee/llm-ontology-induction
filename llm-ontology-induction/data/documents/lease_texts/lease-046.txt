This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Robinson Group (the "Owner") and April Nguyen, operating as Osborne & Associates (the "Tenant"). 

The premises being leased is located at 4391 Post Oak Blvd, Houston, TX. This property is an industrial facility spanning 50077 square feet with a ceiling height of 25.5 feet and 14 loading docks. The Lessee agrees to occupy the space for a term beginning on September 15, 2023, and concluding on September 15, 2024.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Owner a total sum of $44,738.48 for the duration of the term. Payments shall be made in accordance with the schedule agreed upon by the parties.

Regarding adjustments and security, the following terms shall apply: 
Any increases to the financial obligations of the Tenant during the term of this agreement shall be calculated based on a fixed percentage rate as determined by the Landlord. Additionally, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $25,997.14 to be held by the Robinson Group as a guarantee for the performance of the Tenant's obligations under this agreement. This sum shall be returned to the Tenant within thirty days of the conclusion of the lease, provided that no damages have occurred to the property beyond normal wear and tear and all financial obligations have been satisfied.

April Nguyen agrees to maintain the premises in good condition for the duration of the stay. Any disputes regarding this contract shall be directed to the Robinson Group at robinson@owner5mail.com.
