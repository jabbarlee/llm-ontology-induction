This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Johnson Holdings (hereinafter referred to as the Landlord) and Jonathan White, acting on behalf of Liu Group (the Tenant). The parties agree to the rental of the office space located at 8935 Bellaire Blvd, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall commence on May 20, 2024, and shall conclude on May 20, 2026. The Lessee agrees to pay to the Landlord as payment for the premises the sum of $9,563.13 per month.

Regarding the financial terms of this agreement, the parties have agreed to the following:

1. PAYMENT ADJUSTMENTS: The monthly sum due for the premises shall remain fixed at the initial rate of $9,563.13 for the duration of the term. There shall be no periodic adjustments or increases applied to this payment amount over the course of the occupancy.

2. HELD FUNDS: Upon the signing of this document, the Tenant shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $24,272.18. This amount shall be held by Johnson Holdings as a guarantee against any damages to the property or failure to perform the obligations set forth in this agreement. These funds will be returned to the Lessee following the conclusion of the term, provided the premises are surrendered in good condition, minus any necessary deductions for repairs or unpaid charges.

The Tenant, Jonathan White, acknowledges that this space is intended for office-professional use and agrees to abide by all building regulations. The Lessee is responsible for providing their own liability insurance for their operations within the property. If any portion of this agreement is found to be in conflict with local laws, the remainder of the lease shall stay in full effect.

Signed this 20th day of May, 2024.
