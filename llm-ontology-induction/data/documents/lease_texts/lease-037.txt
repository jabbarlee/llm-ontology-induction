This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into on this day between Megan Mcclain (the "Landlord") and Kevin Hall, representing Walker Partners (the "Lessee"). The parties agree to the following terms regarding the industrial property located at 6968 Main St, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall begin on July 20, 2023, and shall continue until July 20, 2024. During this term, the Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a total sum of $24,214.97 for the occupancy of the space.

Regarding the financial obligations and adjustments, the following terms shall apply: The Lessee shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $5,681.36 to be held as a good-faith payment for the performance of this agreement. Furthermore, the agreed-upon payment total shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase as discussed and agreed upon by the parties to account for market adjustments over the period of the lease.

The Lessee, Kevin Hall, accepts the premises in their current condition for use as a restaurant-food service operation. The Landlord, Megan Mcclain, warrants that she has the authority to enter into this agreement for the property described above. Any notices regarding this lease should be directed to the respective contact emails provided by the parties.
