This lease agreement is made and entered into by and between Rogers & Associates (the "Landlord") and Thomas Ramos (the "Tenant"), representing Ramirez Partners. The parties hereby agree to the rental of the retail storefront located at 3219 Richmond Ave, Houston, TX, consisting of 7852 square feet of space. The term of this agreement shall begin on March 28, 2023, and shall continue in full effect until March 28, 2028.

The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $11,435.13 per month for the duration of the initial occupancy. 

Provisions for Adjustments and Collateral:
1. Annual Adjustments: The base monthly payment specified herein shall be subject to an annual CPI-indexed adjustment. On each anniversary of the commencement date, the monthly payment shall be recalculated based on the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index to ensure the value of the payment remains consistent with market fluctuations.
2. Performance Assurance: Upon the execution of this agreement, the Tenant shall deliver to Rogers & Associates the sum of $22,882.61. This amount shall be held by the Landlord to ensure the faithful performance of the Lessee’s obligations under this lease. These funds may be used by the Landlord to cover any damages to the property or to remedy any defaults in payment occurring during the term. Any remaining balance shall be returned to Thomas Ramos following the expiration of the lease and a satisfactory inspection of the premises.

The parties acknowledge that this document constitutes the full agreement between the Landlord and the Tenant regarding the property.
