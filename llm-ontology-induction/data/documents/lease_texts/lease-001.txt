This commercial lease agreement is entered into by Wagner & Associates, the landlord, and Mr. Philip Cannon, the Tenant, regarding the office space located at 7533 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX. 

The Lessee agrees to take possession of the premises for a term beginning on April 1, 2022, and continuing through April 1, 2027. For the use of this space, the Tenant shall pay to Wagner & Associates the sum of $2,072.72 per month. 

Regarding the financial terms of this agreement, the following provisions apply: 

1. Financial Adjustments: There shall be no annual increase or adjustments to the monthly payments throughout the duration of this five-year term; the payment remains fixed at the initial rate agreed upon.

2. Initial Funds: Upon execution of this document, the Lessee shall provide to the landlord the sum of $31,534.86. This sum will be held by Wagner & Associates throughout the term of the tenancy to cover any potential damages or breaches of the agreement, and will be returned to the Tenant following the successful expiration of the lease, provided the property is left in satisfactory condition.

Mr. Philip Cannon agrees to maintain the office space in good standing for the entirety of the term. All notices regarding this agreement should be sent to the parties at their respective email addresses provided in our records.
