COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into by Matthew Gardner (the Landlord) and Elizabeth Fowler (the Tenant). The space being leased is located at 2824 Studemont St, Houston, TX. This property is intended for retail use by the Gaines Group.

The term of this lease shall commence on March 23, 2023, and shall continue for five years, concluding on March 23, 2028. 

The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord a sum of $32,076.99 per year for the use of the premises. Regarding the payment structure, the annual payment shall be adjusted annually based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to account for changes in the economy. Additionally, Elizabeth Fowler shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $9,214.93 at the time of signing to be held as a good-faith payment to cover any potential damages or breaches of the agreement during the lease period.

The Tenant shall be responsible for keeping the interior of the premises in good order. Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be settled in accordance with Texas law.
