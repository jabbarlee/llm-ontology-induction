This Commercial Lease Agreement is made and entered into by and between Matthew Gardner (the Landlord) and Carlos Walls, representing the Caldwell Group (the Tenant).

The property subject to this agreement is located at 3239 Kirby Dr, Houston, TX. The Lessee agrees to occupy this retail space for a term beginning on February 16, 2027, and concluding on February 16, 2028.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a sum of $37,166.83 as the base financial consideration for the premises. 

Regarding adjustments and protections, the following terms apply:
1. Annual Adjustments: The base payment shall be subject to a CPI-indexed increase, calculated annually based on the published Consumer Price Index to reflect changes in economic conditions.
2. Initial Funds: Upon signing this agreement, the Lessee shall provide a sum of $12,847.68 to be held by the Landlord as protection against any potential damages to the property or failure to fulfill the financial obligations set forth in this contract. This amount shall be held for the duration of the term and returned within a reasonable timeframe following the expiration of the lease, provided the property is left in satisfactory condition.

The Lessee shall use the premises solely for the purposes of their medical business. All payments should be directed to the Landlord via the contact information provided in our records. Failure to adhere to these terms may result in the termination of the right to occupy the premises.
