This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Javier Johnson (the "Landlord") and Shawn Arroyo (the "Lessee"). The Landlord agrees to lease to the Tenant the office space located at 6047 Hempstead Rd, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall begin on September 9, 2023, and shall continue for a period of five years, concluding on September 9, 2028.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a monthly payment of $25,561.30 for the use of the premises. 

Additional Terms and Conditions:

1. Adjustments to Payment: Commencing on the first anniversary of this agreement and occurring annually thereafter, the base monthly payment shall be increased by a fixed percentage of 3% over the prior year’s rate.

2. Initial Funds Held: Upon the execution of this agreement, the Lessee shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $15,066.62. This amount shall be held by the Landlord to ensure the faithful performance of the terms of this lease and shall be returned to the Tenant following the expiration of the term, provided the premises are returned in satisfactory condition. 

This document serves as the primary agreement for the occupancy of the office property by Parker & Associates. Any modifications or future adjustments to this arrangement may be handled via separate written addendum.
