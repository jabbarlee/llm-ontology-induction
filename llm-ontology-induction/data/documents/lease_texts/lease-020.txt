This Lease Agreement is made and entered into by and between Matthew Gardner (the "Landlord") and Joshua Baker (the "Lessee").

The Landlord agrees to lease to the Tenant the office space located at 4662 Main St, Houston, TX. The term of this agreement shall begin on April 28, 2029, and shall continue for exactly two years, concluding on April 28, 2031. 

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a base monthly payment of $17,157.16 for the use of the property. Regarding future adjustments to this payment, the parties agree to the following clause: The base monthly rate shall be subject to a CPI-indexed increase, calculated annually based on the published Consumer Price Index, to be applied on each anniversary of the start date. 

Furthermore, upon signing this agreement, Joshua Baker shall provide a sum of $19,878.29 to be held by the Landlord to cover any potential damages to the property or defaults in payment. This amount shall be returned to the Lessee within thirty days of the expiration of the lease, provided the property is left in satisfactory condition. 

The Lessee shall use the premises for the purpose of operating their business, Anderson & Associates. This document constitutes the full agreement between Matthew Gardner and the Tenant regarding the occupation of the premises.
