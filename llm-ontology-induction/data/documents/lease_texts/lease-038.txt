This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into on this day between Robinson Partners (the "Landlord") and Eric Carney, representing Blair Holdings (the "Tenant"). The property subject to this lease is located at 3446 Main St, Houston, TX.

The Lessee agrees to occupy the industrial premises beginning on September 2, 2024. This lease agreement shall remain in full effect for a duration of five years, concluding on September 2, 2029.

1. Financial Terms: The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a base monthly payment of $21,271.51. This base amount shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase of 3% per annum, applied on each anniversary of the commencement date.

2. Security Funds: Upon the execution of this agreement, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $6,001.29 to be held by the Landlord. This amount serves as a guarantee for the faithful performance of the terms contained herein. These funds shall be returned to the Lessee within thirty days following the expiration of the lease, provided the property is returned in good condition, less any necessary deductions for damages beyond reasonable wear and tear.

The Tenant, Eric Carney, shall be responsible for all professional office activities conducted on the premises. Robinson Partners and the Tenant agree that this document serves as the primary agreement for the occupancy of the site. Any modifications to this arrangement, such as the previously discussed amendment lease, shall be considered supplemental to these terms.
