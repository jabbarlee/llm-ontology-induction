This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Johnson Holdings (the "Landlord") and Phillip Ryan (the "Tenant"). The premises being leased are located at 4425 Heights Blvd, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall begin on October 2, 2024, and shall conclude on October 2, 2025. 

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord for the use of the property the total sum of $39,526.95. This payment is to be made in accordance with the agreed-upon schedule.

Regarding financial adjustments and security requirements, the parties agree to the following: There shall be no periodic increase or escalation applied to the payments due under this lease. Furthermore, the Lessee shall provide to the Landlord a sum of $14,226.59 to be held as a good-faith guarantee against any damage to the property or breach of this agreement, to be returned at the conclusion of the term provided the space is surrendered in good condition.

The Lessee, doing business as Lynch LLC, acknowledges that they are entering into this agreement for the use of the industrial property for professional office purposes. The Landlord and Phillip Ryan both agree to abide by the terms set forth herein throughout the duration of the occupancy.
