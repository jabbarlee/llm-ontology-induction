This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Abigail Shaffer, hereafter referred to as the Landlord, and Nicole Brown, who shall be referred to as the Lessee. The parties agree to the rental of the office space located at 444 Fannin St, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall begin on October 12, 2023, and shall continue for exactly five years, concluding on October 12, 2028.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord for the use of the premises at a rate of $28,344.05 per payment period. Regarding the financial terms of this agreement, the following clauses shall apply:

1. Financial Adjustments: The base payment amount specified herein shall remain fixed for the duration of the term, and no annual or periodic increases or cost-of-living adjustments shall be applied to the payment amount. 

2. Damage Assurance: Upon the execution of this agreement, the Lessee shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $40,356.87. This sum is to be held by the Landlord as a guarantee against any damages to the property or breach of the terms of this lease during the residency of Riley Holdings. This amount may be returned to the Tenant after the premises have been vacated and inspected, provided no deductions are required to cover repair costs or unpaid balances.

The Lessee agrees to maintain the premises in good order and use the space strictly for the purposes of their warehouse-logistics operations. The Tenant acknowledges that they have inspected the property and accept it in its current condition.
