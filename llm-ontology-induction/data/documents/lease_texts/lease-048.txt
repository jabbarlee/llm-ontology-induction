This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Blair & Associates (the "Landlord") and Alexandra Le, who is operating as Maddox Partners (the "Lessee"). The parties agree to the rental of the industrial property located at 690 Beltway 8 Frontage, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall begin on July 23, 2022, and shall continue for a period of exactly two years, concluding on July 23, 2024. For the use of the premises, Tenant agrees to pay a monthly sum of $28,897.15.

The Lessee agrees to the following financial terms regarding adjustments and security:

1. Annual Adjustments: The monthly payments required under this agreement shall be subject to a CPI-indexed escalation. Adjustments will be calculated based on the Consumer Price Index and applied annually on the anniversary of the commencement date to ensure the value of the payment remains consistent with market inflation.

2. Security Funds: Upon the execution of this agreement, Alexandra Le shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $30,398.41. This amount shall be held by Blair & Associates as a security deposit to ensure the full and faithful performance of the Lessee’s obligations under this lease. This deposit may be used to cover any damages to the property or unpaid financial obligations upon the vacation of the premises, provided such usage is consistent with state law.

The Tenant is responsible for maintaining the property in good order throughout the term of this lease. All notices regarding this agreement should be sent to the parties at their provided email contacts.
