This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Johnson Holdings (the "Landlord") and Elizabeth Fowler (the "Tenant"), doing business as Gaines Group.

The parties hereby agree to the lease of the retail premises located at 4961 Kirby Dr, Houston, TX. The term of this agreement shall begin on March 26, 2022, and shall continue in full force for three years, concluding on March 26, 2025. 

The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $24,908.05 per month for the duration of the occupancy. Regarding the financial terms of this agreement, there shall be no adjustment or increase to the monthly payment amount during the term of this lease. Furthermore, the Tenant shall provide a payment of $17,934.55 to be held by the Landlord as a good-faith fund against potential damages or defaults under this agreement. This sum shall be held for the duration of the lease and returned to the Tenant after the premises are surrendered, provided all terms have been met.

The Tenant, representing the medical industry, acknowledges that the property is situated in a high-traffic strip mall environment. The Lessee accepts the premises in their current condition as of the date of possession. All notices to Johnson Holdings shall be directed to johnson@owner7mail.com, and all notices to the Tenant shall be directed to contact8@gaines.com. 

This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
