This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Christopher Bernard, as the Landlord, and Elizabeth Fowler, representing Gaines Group, as the Tenant. The parties agree that the Lessee shall occupy the retail space located at 1060 Post Oak Blvd, Houston, TX, beginning on December 2, 2022, and the term of this agreement shall continue until December 2, 2025.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $6,648.25 per month for the duration of the occupancy. Regarding adjustments to this payment, the parties agree to a fixed percentage increase scheduled annually on the anniversary of the move-in date. Furthermore, Elizabeth Fowler shall provide to the Landlord a payment of $41,942.15 upon the execution of this contract, which shall be held as a good-faith fund to cover any potential damages to the property or defaults in payment during the term, to be returned within a reasonable timeframe after the Tenant vacates the premises, provided the space is left in good order.

The Lessee is responsible for maintaining the storefront in a manner consistent with the surrounding strip mall properties. This agreement represents the entire understanding between the parties regarding the use of the 26,336 square foot premises.
