This Lease Agreement is made effective this 16th day of April, 2027, by and between Lindsey Roman (the "Landlord") and Edward Novak, operating as Higgins & Associates (the "Tenant"). 

The Landlord hereby leases to the Lessee the office space located at 109 Fannin St, Houston, TX. The term of this lease shall commence on April 16, 2027, and shall continue for a period of exactly three years, concluding on April 16, 2030.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $21,152.96 per month for the use of the premises. Regarding future adjustments to the payment obligations, the parties agree that the monthly fee shall be subject to a CPI-indexed increase, calculated annually based on the published Consumer Price Index to reflect changes in the cost of living. Furthermore, Edward Novak shall provide to the Landlord a sum of $25,839.74 to be held as a good-faith guarantee against any damages to the property or failure to perform under the terms of this agreement. This sum will be returned to the Tenant upon the successful conclusion of the lease, provided the property is vacated in good order.

The Lessee is entitled to utilize the office space for the purpose of restaurant-food service business operations. The Landlord and the Lessee agree that this document represents the full understanding of the parties regarding the occupation of 109 Fannin St, Houston, TX.
