This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Matthew Gardner (the Landlord) and Joshua Baker (the Tenant), doing business as Anderson & Associates. This agreement covers the office space located at 4662 Main St, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall begin on April 29, 2024, and shall conclude on April 28, 2029.

The Lessee agrees to pay a monthly payment of $16,227.90 for the use of the premises. Regarding future adjustments to this payment, the parties agree that the base payment shall be subject to a CPI-indexed increase, to be calculated annually based on the published Consumer Price Index for the region. Furthermore, upon signing this agreement, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $19,878.29 to be held by Matthew Gardner as a guarantee for the full and faithful performance of all terms and conditions of this lease; this sum shall be returned to Joshua Baker at the conclusion of the term provided the property is returned in good order, less any necessary deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear.

The Tenant agrees to maintain the premises in a clean and orderly fashion throughout the duration of their occupancy. All payments should be directed to the Landlord via the contact methods previously discussed.
