This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Rogers & Associates (“Landlord”) and Thomas Ramos (“Tenant”) for the industrial premises located at 2188 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall commence on August 20, 2024, and shall continue for a period of three years, concluding on August 20, 2027. Ramirez Partners shall occupy the premises for the purpose of retail-related business operations.

Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a monthly base payment of $3,403.51, due on the first of each month. 

Regarding financial adjustments and security requirements, the following terms shall apply:
1. Annual Adjustments: The base payment specified herein shall be subject to an annual CPI-indexed adjustment. Landlord will notify the Lessee in writing of any such adjustments at least thirty days prior to the anniversary of the commencement date.
2. Initial Hold: Upon signing this agreement, the Tenant shall deliver to the Landlord a sum of $32,399.10. This sum shall be held by Rogers & Associates as a performance guarantee to cover potential damages to the property or defaults in payment. The Landlord shall return the balance of these funds to the Tenant within thirty days following the expiration of the lease, provided all obligations have been met.

The Lessee accepts the property in its current condition, including the existing loading docks and vertical clearance, acknowledging that the space is sufficient for their intended use. All payments are to be directed to the contact provided by the ownership trust.
