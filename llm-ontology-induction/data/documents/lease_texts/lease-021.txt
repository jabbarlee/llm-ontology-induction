COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is entered into by Robinson Group, the landlord, and Rebecca Henderson, doing business as Rodriguez & Associates, the Tenant. The parties agree to the following terms regarding the property located at 9455 Fannin St, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall begin on February 19, 2024, and shall conclude on February 19, 2029. 

The Lessee agrees to pay a monthly payment of $2,253.53 for the use of the premises. Commencing on the first anniversary of this agreement, the monthly payment shall be adjusted annually based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to account for inflation and market changes. 

At the time of signing, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $14,840.34 to be held by the Robinson Group. This amount will be held throughout the duration of the term to ensure the faithful performance of the terms and conditions herein. The landlord may utilize these funds to cover any unpaid costs or damages to the property, but shall return the balance to the Tenant following the move-out inspection and return of keys, provided all other obligations under this agreement have been satisfied.

The Tenant, Rebecca Henderson, agrees to maintain the premises in good condition and shall comply with all local ordinances governing the use of the retail space. 

Signed this 19th day of February, 2024.
