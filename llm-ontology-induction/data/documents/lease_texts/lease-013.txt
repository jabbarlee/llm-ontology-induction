COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made by and between Johnson Holdings (hereinafter referred to as "the Landlord") and Jonathan White, doing business as Liu Group (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant").

The Landlord agrees to lease to the Lessee the office space located at 8935 Bellaire Blvd, Houston, TX. The term of this agreement shall begin on May 20, 2026, and shall continue for a period of exactly one year, concluding on May 20, 2027.

The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord a monthly sum of $10,464.20 for the duration of the occupancy. Regarding the financial obligations of the Lessee, the following terms shall apply:

1. Payment Adjustments: There shall be no periodic increases or escalations applied to the base payment amount during the term of this lease; the monthly payment shall remain fixed at the initial rate throughout the duration of this agreement.

2. Performance Guarantee: Upon execution of this document, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $24,272.18 to the Landlord. This amount is to be held as a guarantee for the faithful performance of the Lessee’s obligations under this lease. This sum may be applied by the Landlord to cover any unpaid costs or damages caused to the premises during the tenancy, and any remaining balance will be returned to the Tenant following the successful surrender of the premises, provided all terms have been met.

The Tenant agrees to use the property solely for professional office purposes. The Landlord and the Lessee agree that this document represents the full understanding of the parties regarding the occupation of the space.
