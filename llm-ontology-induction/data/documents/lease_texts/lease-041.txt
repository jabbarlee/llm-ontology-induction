This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Megan Mcclain (the "Landlord") and Andrea Young (the "Tenant"), representing Whitehead LLC, regarding the industrial premises located at 252 Yale St, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall commence on April 18, 2023, and shall continue in full force until April 18, 2024. 

The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $33,015.23 as the base payment for the premises. 

**Payment Adjustments and Security Provisions:**
The base payment described herein shall be subject to a CPI-indexed increase, adjusted annually based on the Consumer Price Index to reflect market changes. Furthermore, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $7,140.85 to be held by Megan Mcclain to cover any potential damages or breaches of the agreement during the occupancy period. This amount shall be returned to the Tenant within a reasonable timeframe following the conclusion of the lease, provided the property is returned in good condition.

The Tenant, Andrea Young, agrees to maintain the property in accordance with industrial standards suitable for the space, which includes access to the four loading docks on site. Both parties acknowledge their commitment to these terms by signing below.
