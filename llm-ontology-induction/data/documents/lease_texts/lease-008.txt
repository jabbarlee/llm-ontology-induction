This Lease Agreement is made and entered into by and between Walker Group (hereinafter referred to as the "Landlord") and Tammy Allison, representing Huff Group (the "Tenant").

The Landlord agrees to lease to the Lessee, and the Lessee agrees to lease from the Landlord, the commercial office space located at 4046 Hempstead Rd, Houston, TX. This premises comprises approximately 38,682 square feet of office space across two floors, including the use of 53 designated parking spaces.

The term of this lease shall commence on March 14, 2028, and shall continue in full force and effect until March 14, 2031.

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $38,893.52 as the recurring payment for the premises. Regarding the payment structure and financial protections, the parties agree to the following terms:

Payment Adjustments and Security: There shall be no annual increases, cost-of-living adjustments, or other escalations applied to the base payment amount throughout the duration of this three-year term. Upon the execution of this agreement, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $9,296.73 to be held by the Walker Group. This amount will be retained as collateral for the faithful performance of the terms of this lease and may be applied toward any damages to the property or unpaid balances upon the conclusion of the tenancy.

The Tenant is responsible for ensuring all utility accounts are transferred into their name prior to taking possession. This agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Walker Group and Tammy Allison regarding the use of the premises at 4046 Hempstead Rd.
