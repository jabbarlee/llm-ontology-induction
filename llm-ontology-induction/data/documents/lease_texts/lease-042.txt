This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Megan Mcclain (hereinafter referred to as the Landlord) and Shawn Arroyo, doing business as Parker & Associates (hereinafter referred to as the Tenant). The Landlord agrees to lease to the Tenant the industrial property located at 8908 Kirby Dr, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall begin on March 20, 2023, and shall continue in full force and effect until March 20, 2028. 

The Lessee agrees to pay to the Landlord a monthly sum of $13,889.48 as consideration for the use of the premises. With respect to adjustments in the monthly payment, there shall be no annual increase or cost-of-living escalation applied to the base rate during the term of this agreement. 

Upon the signing of this agreement, the Tenant shall provide to the Landlord the sum of $43,994.10. This payment serves as a good-faith deposit to ensure the performance of all terms and conditions herein. The Landlord shall hold these funds for the duration of the lease and may apply them toward any damages to the property or unpaid balances upon the expiration or early termination of this lease, provided that any remaining balance is returned to the Tenant within a reasonable timeframe thereafter.

The Tenant acknowledges that they have inspected the premises and accepts the property in its current condition. The Lessee shall be responsible for all utilities consumed at the property.
