This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Davis & Associates, the landlord, and Daniel Ryan, representing Brennan Holdings, the Lessee. This agreement governs the use of the retail property located at 9116 Richmond Ave, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall commence on May 21, 2022, and shall continue in full force for a duration of one year, concluding on May 21, 2023. 

The Tenant agrees to pay the landlord for the use of the premises in the total amount of $34,432.03 for the duration of the term. Regarding payment adjustments and security funds, the following terms apply: The total sum due shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase as negotiated by the parties to account for standard market adjustments over the period. Furthermore, the Lessee shall provide to Davis & Associates a sum of $10,572.51 to be held as a guarantee against any potential damages or breaches of this agreement, which shall be returned to the Tenant upon the successful conclusion of the lease, provided the property is left in good standing.

As the Lessee, Daniel Ryan agrees to maintain the storefront in accordance with local retail standards. The landlord, Davis & Associates, shall be notified via the email address provided should any significant structural issues arise that would hinder the normal operation of the retail business.
