This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Davis Partners (the "Landlord") and Mr. James Brown (the "Lessee"), regarding the office space located at 9107 Post Oak Blvd, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall commence on February 3, 2023, and shall continue in full force until February 3, 2028. 

The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $23,258.22 per month as consideration for the use of the premises. 

Additional Terms:
1. Annual Adjustments: The monthly payment amount set forth above shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase of 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date throughout the term of this agreement. 
2. Initial Funds: Upon execution of this lease, the Lessee shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $8,345.57 to be held to ensure the faithful performance of the terms and conditions herein. This amount shall be held by Davis Partners throughout the duration of the tenancy and returned to the Tenant within thirty days of the expiration of the lease, provided no damages or arrears are found. 

Mr. James Brown, operating as Martin Partners, accepts the property in its current condition. The Lessee shall be responsible for all utility costs associated with the daily operation of the retail business, provided such use remains consistent with the character of the building.
