This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into on this day between Megan Mcclain (the "Landlord") and Jonathan White, operating as Liu Group, for the industrial premises located at 4102 Washington Ave, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall commence on February 2, 2023, and shall continue in full force for exactly three years, concluding on February 2, 2026. The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord the sum of $11,781.78 per month for the use of the 20,176 square foot facility.

Financial Terms:
The Tenant agrees to provide the Landlord with a sum of $14,997.51 upon the signing of this document to be held as a good-faith payment against any damages or defaults. Furthermore, the monthly payment shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase, which shall be applied automatically on each anniversary of the move-in date throughout the duration of the term.

The Lessee shall be responsible for all standard maintenance of the internal premises and agrees to abide by all local ordinances regarding the use of the industrial space. If the Tenant falls behind on payments, the Landlord reserves the right to exercise all available legal remedies to recover the balance due.
