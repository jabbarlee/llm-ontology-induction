This Commercial Lease Agreement is made between Davis Partners (the "Landlord") and Jesse Mckay, doing business as Rodriguez & Associates, regarding the office space located at 1233 Bellaire Blvd, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall commence on April 27, 2026, and shall continue for a period of exactly two years, concluding on April 27, 2028. During this term, the Tenant shall pay to the Landlord the sum of $12,508.24 per month as compensation for the use of the premises.

Regarding the financial terms and conditions of this occupancy: The Lessee shall provide a sum of $8,336.22 to be held as a damage and performance bond throughout the duration of the agreement. This sum will be returned to the Tenant upon the expiration of the lease, provided the property is returned in good condition. Furthermore, the monthly payment amount specified above shall increase by a fixed percentage of 3% on each anniversary of the move-in date for the duration of the occupancy.

Jesse Mckay agrees to use the premises solely for professional office purposes. The Landlord and the Lessee agree that all utilities and maintenance of the interior of the space are the responsibility of the Tenant. The Tenant agrees to abide by all building rules and local regulations as applicable to the property.
