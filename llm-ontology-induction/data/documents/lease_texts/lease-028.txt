This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by and between Davis & Associates, an LLC, and the tenant, Daniel Ryan, representing Brennan Holdings. The parties hereby agree to the leasing of the retail space located at 9116 Richmond Ave, Houston, TX, which consists of 4,815 square feet in a strip mall setting.

The term of this lease shall commence on May 21, 2023, and shall continue for exactly one year, concluding on May 21, 2024. For the use and occupation of said premises, the Lessee agrees to pay the owner the sum of $36,039.94 for the duration of the term.

Regarding financial requirements and adjustments, the parties agree to the following terms:

The Tenant shall provide a payment in the amount of $10,572.51 to be held by the owner to cover any potential damages or breaches of this agreement, which shall be returned at the end of the term provided the space is left in good standing. Furthermore, the total payment obligation shall be subject to a fixed percentage increase as negotiated between the parties to account for market adjustments throughout the duration of the residency.

Davis & Associates agrees to provide the space for the Tenant’s retail operations as described. Daniel Ryan agrees to maintain the property in a professional manner. This document serves as the binding agreement between the landlord and the Tenant.
