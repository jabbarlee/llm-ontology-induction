This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Lindsey Roman (the Landlord) and Amber Kidd, acting on behalf of Callahan LLC (the Tenant). The property being leased is the retail space located at 2679 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease shall commence on December 4, 2024, and shall continue for exactly three years, concluding on December 4, 2027. 

The Lessee agrees to pay the Landlord a base monthly payment of $28,484.23 for the duration of the occupancy. There will be no adjustments or increases to this monthly payment throughout the duration of this agreement. 

Upon the signing of this document, the Tenant shall provide a sum of $35,309.58 to be held by the Landlord. This sum shall be kept as a guarantee against any damages to the premises or defaults by the Tenant during the term. The Landlord will hold these funds in a non-interest-bearing account and will return the balance of said funds to the Lessee within thirty days of the expiration of this lease, provided the property is returned in good condition.

The Tenant, Amber Kidd, shall use the premises for the purpose of operating a restaurant or food service business. The Landlord and Tenant agree to abide by the terms set forth herein.
