This commercial lease agreement is made by and between Megan Mcclain (hereinafter referred to as the Landlord) and Kevin Hall, operating as Walker Partners (the Tenant). The parties agree to the following terms regarding the property located at 9849 Richmond Ave, Houston, TX.

The term of this lease begins on May 18, 2023, and shall continue until May 18, 2024. During this period, the Lessee agrees to pay a monthly sum of $13,993.91 for the use of the retail premises. 

Regarding payments and financial protections, the parties agree to the following: 
The Tenant shall provide the Landlord with a payment of $12,486.13 to be held for the duration of the term as a security deposit to cover any potential damages or breaches of this agreement. Furthermore, because the economic conditions may change, the monthly payment obligation shall be subject to a CPI-indexed adjustment, meaning the amount due will be modified annually in accordance with the Consumer Price Index to reflect inflation or changes in the cost of living. 

The Tenant shall occupy the property for the purposes of their restaurant-food service business. The Landlord and Kevin Hall both acknowledge that the premises consist of a standalone structure and agree to adhere to the terms set forth in this document. Any notices regarding this agreement should be directed to the Landlord at megan@owner3mail.com or the Tenant at contact18@walker.com.
