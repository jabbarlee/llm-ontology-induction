This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into by Megan Mcclain, as the landlord, and Alexandra Le, who is operating as Maddox Partners, the Tenant. The subject of this agreement is the industrial property located at 7039 Beltway 8 Frontage, Houston, TX.

The Lessee agrees to occupy the premises for a term beginning on September 5, 2024, and concluding on September 5, 2025. In consideration for the use of the space, the Tenant shall pay a total sum of $37,350.97 for the duration of the term.

Regarding the financial obligations and adjustments, the parties agree to the following: 
"The Lessee shall provide a payment of $23,145.10 to the landlord upon signing, which shall be held as a guarantee against any damages or defaults under this agreement. Furthermore, the base payment amount shall be subject to adjustment throughout the term based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) to ensure the payment remains consistent with current economic conditions."

Alexandra Le agrees to maintain the warehouse-logistics facility in good order during the period of occupancy. The Lessee shall be responsible for all utilities consumed at the premises. This agreement represents the full understanding between Megan Mcclain and the Tenant regarding the space at the Beltway 8 location.
