COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made this day between Christopher Bernard, the Landlord, and Megan Joyce, who is doing business as Owens Partners, the Lessee. The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant the industrial property located at 2244 Main St, Houston, TX. 

The term of this lease shall commence on December 23, 2024, and shall continue for a period of three years, concluding on December 23, 2027.

The Tenant agrees to pay to the Landlord the sum of $20,296.66 per month as consideration for the use of the premises. There shall be no periodic increases applied to the monthly payment amount throughout the duration of this agreement. 

Upon the execution of this document, the Tenant shall provide the Landlord with the sum of $25,666.70. This amount shall be held by the Landlord to ensure the Tenant’s full and faithful performance of all terms and conditions set forth in this lease. The Landlord may, at his discretion, apply all or any portion of these funds to remedy any default by the Lessee, or to repair any damage caused to the property during the term.

The Tenant is responsible for all activities conducted within the space and agrees to maintain the premises in good condition throughout the duration of their occupancy. All notices regarding this agreement should be directed to the contact information provided by the respective parties.
